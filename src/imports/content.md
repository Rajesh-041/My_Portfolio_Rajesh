## Scene 01 — MERGIFY
**Unified Campus Management Platform**

*Logline:* One platform, every part of campus life — attendance, safety, announcements, and AI-guided studying, all in sync.

**Overview**
A role-based campus management platform for students and faculty, integrating attendance tracking, announcements, safety reporting, and AI-powered study planning with real-time synchronization.

**Stack:** React · Vite · Tailwind CSS · Firebase · Firestore

---

## Scene 02 — HomeChef Connect
**Community Food Exchange Platform**

*Logline:* Home kitchens, meet your neighborhood — a full-stack marketplace connecting home chefs with the people looking for a home-cooked meal.

**Overview**
A full-stack food-sharing platform connecting home chefs with customers through location-based discovery, secure authentication, payment processing, image management, and order tracking.

**Stack:** React · Node.js · Express.js · MongoDB · Razorpay · Cloudinary

---

## Scene 03 — SafeGuard AI
**AI-Powered Fall Detection Dashboard**

*Logline:* A quiet system watching for the moment someone needs help — and reacting before it's too late.

**Overview**
A real-time, AI-assisted monitoring dashboard capable of processing webcam feeds and generating emergency alerts using confidence-based prediction validation.

**Stack:** React · TypeScript · Vite · Axios

---

## Scene 04 — ATTENDO
**Facial Recognition Attendance System**

*Logline:* No cards, no signatures — just a face, recognized in real time.

**Overview**
A contactless attendance system using facial recognition technology for automated student identification, attendance recording, reporting, and analytics.

**Stack:** React · face-api.js · Firebase Firestore
